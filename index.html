The session's biggest surprise was oil: after the morning described WTI as "declining on ME de-escalation," AP News broke after 4 PM that the U.S. had begun air strikes against Iran following the Army helicopter shoot-down, reversing crude from intraday lows to close +1.49% at $89.51 — a complete narrative flip that also complicates AAL's next session despite today's +3.60% close. The watchlist scorecard was strong: TOL +5.09% hit the $140–145 KBW-upgrade entry zone precisely, the IWM vs QQQ spread widened by 1.38% confirming the small-cap rotation thesis, and CRWV fell -3.83% on the debt-concern thesis. CBRL delivered the after-hours headline, jumping +9.50% to $39.75 on a Q3 beat and raised FY26 guide. BTC slid -2.74% to $61,697, extending the crypto weakness noted in the morning. All eyes now shift to Wednesday 8:30 AM CPI: a cool print unlocks the AAPL/QQQ long and could stabilize tech; a hot print accelerates the selloff past SPX 7,350 support.
