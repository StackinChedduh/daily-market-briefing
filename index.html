The morning's cautiously bullish bias was partially wrong — indices opened higher then drifted into a modest red close as weak macro data and Fed ambiguity weighed. ADP came in at 98K vs. the ~125K consensus, a meaningful miss that softened the "Q3 re-acceleration" thesis and pulled Nasdaq down 0.66% as chip stocks led the selling. Fed Chair Warsh at Sintra reaffirmed his commitment to price stability without offering policy guidance, which kept rate-sensitive names under pressure. The session's biggest surprise was ESS: despite the premarket −11.6% implosion, the stock recovered entirely and closed +1.50% at $295.96 — the REIT short thesis failed on the day. Standouts on the long side: GIS surged +7.2% on a BMO beat (EPS $0.95 vs. $0.80 est.), NOW climbed +6.2% extending on the IBM partnership catalyst, and MSM beat estimates handily (EPS $1.43 vs. $1.26) closing near $123. WTI held the $68 technical level barely, closing at $68.06 as Iran risk premium continued to fade.
