Downside scenarios
  • Hot Core PCE Jun 25 — Core above 0.3% MoM or YoY failing to decline from 3.3% gives Warsh justification for a July hike. This is the single biggest near-term macro risk. Philly Prices Paid (53.2 today) is a leading signal that upstream inflation hasn't fully cooled.

  • MU miss on Jun 24 — Micron earnings (est EPS $20.05, Rev $35B) is the semiconductor group's reality check. A miss or cautious guide would directly challenge the INTC/MRVL/NVDA rally — the group is priced for perfection. Note also that the Micron CEO sold $36M in stock near all-time highs on Jun 4.
  • Fed Stress Test surprise Jun 24 — If a major bank fails, financials (already -0.91% today) could see additional pressure and credit conditions tighten.
  • SPX 7,500 level failure — A weekly close below 7,420 (prior close) reopens the 7,300 zone and would signal the Iran-deal bounce was a sell-the-news event.
  • Dollar strength continuation — DXY +0.71% today. If Warsh's hawkish posture continues to drive dollar strength, EM assets, commodities, and multinational earnings (already pressured for ACN) face headwinds.
  • ARM downgrade signal — New Street's ARM Neutral downgrade during a semi rally is a canary. If institutional money begins rotating out of the high-multiple semis into cheaper plays (BXP, EPRT, REXR per Scotiabank), the tech-led rally could broaden but the leaders could stall.
