The Framework — full trading rules reference
1. Trade Setup Criteria (only trade when ALL are met)
  • Time window: 9:45–11:30am or 1:30–3:00pm. Avoid the open (chaotic) and final hour (gamma trap).
  • Clear bias: You need a reason — VWAP direction, prior day level test, macro catalyst. "Feels like it'll go up" doesn't count.
  • VIX check: VIX above 20 = wider spreads, faster moves. Adjust size down. VIX below 15 = sluggish, harder to profit on directional plays.
  • No major news in the next 30 min (Fed speakers, economic data).
2. Position Sizing Rules
  • Max per trade: 3-5% of account (~$300-500 on a $10k account)
  • Max 2 trades open simultaneously
  • Max daily loss: 10% — hit it, you're done for the day, no exceptions
3. Entry
  • Buy slightly OTM (1-2 strikes out), not deep OTM lottery tickets
  • Target options with delta 0.30–0.45 — enough sensitivity without paying for pure gamma
  • Check the bid/ask spread — if it's wider than 20% of the mid price, skip it
4. Exit Rules (the part most people skip)

Up 100%+Sell half, let rest run with stop at breakeven
Down 30-40%Cut it. Done.
Approaching 2pmExit regardless of P&L — theta accelerates
5. Trade Journal (non-negotiable)

Log every trade: time, bias/reason, entry delta, P&L, and what you were thinking. This is where the real edge comes from over time.

Session Narrative

What Happened

Stocks snapped a three-day losing streak after the Treasury Department said it would more than double the size of its debt-buyback operation, pulling global bond yields down and easing the pressure that had dragged the S&P 500 to Tuesday's 7,691.76 close. The rally lost steam as the day went on — the Dow was up as much as 0.7% intraday before closing +0.22% — after the 2 PM FOMC minutes showed a far more hawkish committee than assumed: a 9-3 hold with three regional Fed presidents (Cleveland's Hammack, Minneapolis's Kashkari, Dallas's Logan) dissenting in favor of a hike, the first three-way dissent since 2016. The real story under the calm index-level tape was rotation, not resolution: Moderna and Merck exploded (+176.97% and +12.60%) on a "landmark" Phase 3 melanoma-vaccine win, lifting healthcare and small caps (Russell 2000 +0.50%) while multiple session recaps described mega-cap/AI-adjacent tech as the lagging group ("S&P 500 rises despite tech weakness"). Retail earnings undercut this morning's optimism rather than confirming it — Target's beat-and-raise didn't hold (-2.79%), Lowe's cautious DIY-spending outlook weighed as flagged (-1.17%), and TJX sold off sharply on a soft Q3 profit forecast despite its Q2 beat. Fabrinet's earnings-miss rout deepened toward -21% and SanDisk extended its slide toward -9%, both continuing straight through from this morning's premarket weakness with no relief.
