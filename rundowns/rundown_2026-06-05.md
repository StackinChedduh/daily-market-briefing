## 6. Broader Market Themes

**Rotation is the story.** The Dow made a new record (+1.7%) Thursday while Nasdaq sold off. This is not a risk-off-everything tape — it is a "sell AI premium, buy real economy" tape. Financials leading (+2.68%), Healthcare surging (+3.16%), Industrials solid (+1.24%), Real Estate bouncing (+2.13%). Technology crushed (-1.60%) and the AVGO contagion this morning amplifies that.

**"Jobs are fine, Fed stays higher longer."** The 172K print removes any Fed cut urgency for June or July. June CPI next Wednesday (Jun 10) is the next real inflection point. If CPI comes in above the 0.4% MoM consensus (Core), yields spike, NQ cracks harder. If CPI undershoots, tech gets its first reprieve.

**Semiconductor positioning is dangerous.** AVGO -12.59% on guidance. MU CEO selling $36M. Bearish options flow hit MU hard at EOD Thursday (multiple bearish blocks/sweeps across June 12 and June 18 expirations). CIEN down 13.6%. This is sequential weakness, not a one-stock event.

**Bitcoin at a critical support zone.** Benzinga flagged BTC sitting ~$62,000 on the 200-week moving average — the same level that only broke during FTX, Terra, and 3AC collapses. Ethereum off ~7% today. Crypto risk-off correlates with NQ risk-off. Watch for BTC close relative to 200W MA; a weekly close below would be structurally significant.

**PDT rule eliminated.** The $25K minimum for pattern day trading has been abolished, per Benzinga. HOOD insider buy noted earlier this week (one of the biggest in years). HOOD is structurally a winner here as retail day-trading barriers come down.

**Cross-asset:** Gold -0.63%, down with strong dollar on jobs beat. Crude flat at $80.46. Yields probably higher on the NFP print — watch 10Y.
