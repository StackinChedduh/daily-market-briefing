On NDX/NQ terms, a confirmed close back above the 50-day MA (~29,800) is still needed to fully negate this week's technical damage — Tuesday/Wednesday's bounce hasn't done that yet.
- **Biggest risk to the call:** Fed Chair Warsh's Senate Banking Committee testimony at 10 AM ET today (the second half of his debut congressional appearance) plus the 1 PM Beige Book — a hawkish tone despite cooling inflation, or a materially disruptive escalation in the Strait of Hormuz (Trump reportedly vowing "very hard" hits on Iran), could reverse the reclaim quickly.
