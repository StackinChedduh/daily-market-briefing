## 6. Broader Market Themes

**AI Supplier vs. AI Customer Rotation:** The defining Q2 trade was tech giants spending massively on AI infrastructure while semiconductor and infrastructure suppliers captured the gains. Mag7 lost ~$2.3 trillion in market cap during June as the market questioned ROI timelines. Cramer summarized it as "Wall Street now rewarding companies with products in high demand and punishing their customers." MU, INTC, MRVL, AMD, SNDK are the current momentum carriers.

**Rate Sensitivity Re-emerging:** With JOLTS hitting a 2-year high and markets beginning to price a September hike, the spread between rate-sensitive losers (REITs, long-duration) and rate-insensitive (cyclicals, tech suppliers) is widening. ESS's -11.6% move is a canary.

**Oil Risk Premium Fading:** Iran-US diplomatic collapse paradoxically compressed oil today — the market was pricing in continued escalation; ceasefire news removed the war premium. WTI at $68.77 is a fresh headwind for XOM, CVX.

**Q2-to-Q3 Continuity Question:** The strongest quarterly rally since 2020 ends yesterday. The question, as TheStreet Pro's Rev Shark framed it: "is the strength genuine or merely quarter-end markup?" Holiday-shortened week (July 4 Friday) means low volume can exaggerate moves either direction.

**Gold/Silver Weakness:** Both metals down on rising real yield expectations. Gold -0.88% to $4,003 — still above $4K psychologically significant level.
