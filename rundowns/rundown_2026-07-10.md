- **Strait of Hormuz disruption:** A confirmed interruption to tanker traffic (the strait handles roughly a fifth of global oil/LNG flow) would be a far larger oil-supply shock than the current simmering conflict — this remains the single largest tail risk on the board.
- **AI-capex valuation de-rating resumes:** Thursday's chip rebound could prove a single-session bounce rather than a trend change if BofA's "bubble risk" framing regains the narrative — Intel's >20% one-week drawdown earlier this month shows how fast sentiment can flip.
- **SK Hynix debut disappoints:** A weak reception despite 7x oversubscription would be a meaningfully bearish read-through for the entire AI/memory trade heading into next week's bank earnings and the CPI print.
- **Hot CPI (Jul 14) combined with elevated inflation expectations:** NY Fed survey data showing inflation expectations at multi-year highs raises the risk of a more hawkish repricing than the current 79.1% odds of a July hold suggest, especially with oil holding a geopolitical floor.
