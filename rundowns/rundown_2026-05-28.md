## 6. Broader Market Themes

**Geopolitical Risk Premium Repriced:** US military strikes on Iranian positions near the Strait of Hormuz have immediately repriced energy risk. WTI +2.65% to $91 reflects genuine supply-chain anxiety. If the strait is threatened, the inflationary read-through is significant — this could re-complicate the Fed's disinflation path.

**Inflation Picture: Still Improving, but Stalling:** Today's Core PCE MoM at 0.2% is the first "clean" beat in several months. However, the YoY 3.3% reading and the Q1 PCE Price QoQ at 4.5% suggest that while monthly progress is real, the annualized pace remains well above the Fed's 2% target. The GDP revision to 1.6% alongside corporate profits declining 0.4% QoQ (vs. +5.7% expected) is a stagflation-adjacent signal traders will not ignore.

**Earnings Quality Divergence:** The session is revealing clear winners and losers. AI infrastructure names (DELL, MRVL, CBRS, IREN as AI cloud) are working; legacy software (ZS, BRZE) is being punished for any softness. Canadian banks all beat — suggests credit conditions remain stable in North America.

**Cross-Asset Signals:** Gold down -1.48% suggests flight-to-safety trade is not fully engaged (geopolitical fear partly offset by USD strength from yield pressure). BTC -2.86% at ~$73,389 reflects macro risk-off. Crude's surge is the cleanest geopolitical expression.
