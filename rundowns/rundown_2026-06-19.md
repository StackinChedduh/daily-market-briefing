## 6. Broader Market Themes

**Tech/AI crowding at record levels.** BofA June 2026 survey: 80% of fund managers are long global semiconductors — the most crowded single trade in survey history. NVDA at $210 (+2.95% Thursday), SPCX at $185 (-3.56%), and semi ETFs running hot. Crowding at this level historically precedes sharp reversals on negative catalysts. MU earnings Wednesday is the pressure test.

**Iran deal = structural oil headwind.** US and Iran signed an initial agreement ending hostilities, easing sanctions, and reopening the Strait of Hormuz. If implemented, global oil supply constraints ease materially. WTI and energy sector (SIXE -1.69%) already pricing some of this. Longer-term this could keep energy depressed and help inflation cooling.

**Short-term Treasury yields at cycle highs.** The Fed hasn't moved. Hawkish hold = front-end yields staying elevated. This creates a rate-drag on Financials and value, and pressures the cost of carry in leveraged positions. Market is shrugging it off for now on the back of AI enthusiasm, but Core PCE Thursday will test whether that continues.

**Russell's relative strength.** Small caps +2.12% vs Nasdaq +1.91% Thursday. If sustained, this is a healthier broadening signal. Watch whether IWM holds gains into next week's heavy macro data.

**Geopolitical recalibration.** US-Iran deal and Israel-Lebanon tensions moving in opposite directions. Israel vowing to "burn" Lebanon while US-Iran talks in Switzerland were actually postponed (Benzinga notes the ceasefire talks were paused after Israeli strikes intensified). The headline narrative and reality may diverge here.

**IT Services contagion.** ACN's -18% and EPAM's -12.6% signal that enterprise IT spending is not accelerating uniformly. AI enthusiasm may be masking weakness in traditional consulting/services. Monitor INFY, CTSH next week.
