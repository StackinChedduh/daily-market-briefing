## 11. Risks & What Could Break the Thesis

1. **Fed re-acceleration:** If Thursday's initial claims surge or Philly Fed reading (already -0.4) confirms growth slowdown faster than expected, the market could re-test 7,200 quickly. A stagflation narrative (slowing growth + stubborn inflation + hawkish Fed) would be the worst outcome.

2. **Earnings guidance cuts:** CarMax's margin warning is a template. If Accenture or Kroger issues guidance below consensus Thursday, the market's fragile post-FOMC footing could crack further.

3. **Credit / Funding stress:** Hawkish Fed + slowing growth + high yields = stress in leveraged positions. Watch for any credit market signals (IG/HY spreads, VIX above 20).

4. **Geopolitical escalation:** Iran peace deal unfinalized means energy remains at risk. An adverse development could spike WTI and re-ignite inflation fears at exactly the wrong time.

5. **Bull case disruption:** A softer-than-expected Philly Fed read (which already printed terrible at -0.4) could reinforce a "Fed is behind the curve" narrative — forcing a dovish pivot faster than currently priced. VIX staying below 20 and a rally in rate-sensitive sectors (utilities, REIT) would signal this shift.
