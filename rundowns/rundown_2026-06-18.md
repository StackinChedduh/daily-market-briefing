### Macro Narrative

The dominant event was the signed US-Iran Memorandum of Understanding to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, which had been under implicit threat for several weeks. The removal of that risk premium simultaneously crushed energy (oil settled near pre-war prices per Barron's), crushed gold (safe-haven unwind -3.36%), crushed crypto (BTC gave back the Iran-deal bounce to $65K and settled ~$63K), and gave the broad equity market a green tape. The secondary driver was Kevin Warsh's first FOMC meeting as chair. The Fed held rates as expected, but Warsh's tone signaled openness to rate hikes later in 2026, citing trimmed PCE and persistent services inflation. AP News noted "Fed policymakers show support for rate hikes as Warsh reins in guidance." The combination — geopolitical relief + hawkish Fed — explains the cross-asset behavior: risk-on into tech/growth, risk-off for commodities/crypto/defensives.
