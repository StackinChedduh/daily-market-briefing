## 6. Broader Market Themes

**Iran/Oil Decoupling.** WTI crude down nearly 4% on ceasefire optimism while equities surge — classic inflation relief + growth trade. Energy sector faces a headwind even as industrials and tech lead. The divergence can persist as long as the diplomatic narrative holds, but the ceasefire is fragile.

**Quantum Computing Surge.** RGTI, QBTS, NVTS, and related names are in full momentum mode. Quantinuum also priced its IPO today, adding fresh institutional attention. Moves are speculative and fast; these can reverse sharply on sentiment shifts.

**Space Sector Momentum.** RKLB, ASTS, and RDW all lifting on broader space infrastructure optimism. LUNR hitting a 52-week high with a confirmed golden cross.

**DOW 50,000 Milestone.** First time ever. Largely symbolic but attracts attention and can pull retail flows into broadly-held equities and ETFs.

**S&P 500 Eight-Week Winning Streak.** Longest since late 2023. Ten of eleven sectors closed positive last session. Streak faces a test Thursday with PCE data.

**Bitcoin Underperforming.** BTC at ~$77,000 while equities hit records. Bitcoin ETFs saw $105M in net outflows last Friday, Ethereum ETFs $6.7M. When crypto lags a broad equity rally, it can signal that the liquidity driving equities is sector-specific rather than macro-wide.

## 11. Risks & What Could Break the Thesis

1. **Iran ceasefire collapses.** IRGC already shot down a US drone overnight. If fighting resumes, oil spikes, risk assets sell off sharply. This is the #1 tail risk for today's open.

2. **Thursday Core PCE surprise.** Core PCE MoM APR consensus is 0.3%, but YoY remains at 3.2% — still well above target. A hotter print crushes rate cut bets and caps the equity rally heading into June.

3. **GDP Q1 2nd estimate at 0.5%.** A dramatic downward revision from 2.0% initial. If the 2nd estimate comes in even weaker, recession fears resurface and the Goldilocks narrative takes a hit — exactly what JPMorgan is flagging.

4. **Quantum/space parabolic unwind.** RGTI, QBTS, NVTS up 20% pre-market with no fundamental earnings catalyst. One negative headline, profit-taking cascade, or ETF redemption could reverse these intraday.

5. **Bitcoin/crypto underperformance.** BTC at $77K while DOW hits 50K is a notable divergence. If crypto is pricing in something equities are not — liquidity tightening, risk-off under the surface — it is a warning signal worth monitoring.

6. **NVDA/Taiwan compliance risk.** NVIDIA CEO publicly pressing SMCI on Taiwan compliance. Broader semiconductor supply chain headline risk could weigh on the AI trade — the primary driver of this rally.
