## 6. Broader Market Themes

**Semiconductor fragmentation:** The SOX decline wasn't random — it was thesis-driven. Citi questioning whether hyperscalers can demonstrate AI capex ROI to shareholders. Meta announcing it will sell access to its own AI compute (implying buildout is ahead of internal monetization). Apple reportedly exploring Chinese chip sourcing. Any one of these would move the sector; all three at once created a cascade. The question for Monday: is this a sector rotation moment or a crowded-unwind in progress?

**NFP and the growth scare:** A 57K print puts the 3-month average well below stall speed for a 160M-person labor force. Leisure/hospitality -61K has a World Cup seasonal explanation, but revisions (-74K combined to prior months) suggest the trend was already decelerating. The market will have to decide whether this is "bad news is good news" (no hike) or whether it's simply bad news (growth scare, earnings risk).

**Energy deflationary: WTI $67.** Post-Iran nuclear talks, Gulf supply is surging. This directly undermines Warsh's energy-inflation argument for the pause. Lower energy → lower CPI → even less reason to hike. Refinery margins and E&P names will feel the squeeze.

**Factor rotation:** Growth lagged value on the week as rates initially stayed elevated. Post-NFP, the bull steepener favors: long-duration growth (QQQ), financials (yield curve steepener helps banks), gold. Hurt: energy E&P, REITs (higher long-end), utilities.

**Gold's structural bid:** $4,200/oz is a new threshold. Fed on pause, dollar softening, global geopolitical uncertainty still present — gold's macro setup hasn't broken despite rate volatility. Watch $4,150 as support on any Monday giveback.
